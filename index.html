About Anega Sarees.

Anega Sarees is a saree manufacturer, weaver, and direct-to-consumer wholesaler based in Ilampillai, Tamil Nadu, India.

We serve retail buyers and home-based resellers, helping customers connect directly with our saree business for product enquiries and wholesale purchases.

We also offer zero-investment reselling partnerships. Interested resellers can contact us to learn about the process, product availability, pricing and partnership terms.

Our aim is to connect customers with traditional sarees while building lasting relationships with buyers and reselling partners.
